Evelyn and the TimeBand: The Golden Gate Bridge
Open index.html in Chrome, Safari, or Edge. Keep the img and audio folders next to it.
Works on a phone or tablet. Tap "Read to me" to hear the story. Tap any word to hear it from there.
PAUSE: the big round button at the top (green play / orange pause) pauses and plays. It picks up again from the same word. The space bar does the same on a keyboard.
Grown-up menu = gear button (endings found, auto-read option, backup voice).
Real photos in img/photos come from the Library of Congress, the U.S. Navy, the National Park Service, and Wikimedia Commons. Credits are shown under each photo. One opening-day photo is CC BY-SA 3.0 by Mr. Euler via Karen McCutcheon.
